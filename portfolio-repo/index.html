SHEET 02 — ABOUT

About

I'm drawn to problems that sit across disciplines: a lead screw that behaves differently than its spec sheet claims, a vision system thrown off by an unexpected wavelength, a hydrogel that needs to behave like real tissue. My background spans tissue biomechanics, orthopedics, robotics, and electromechanical design, and most of my work has involved taking a mechanism from a rough idea to something that performs reliably under real cycles, real tolerances, and real scrutiny.
